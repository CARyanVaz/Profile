Privacy notice

How personal data from website visits and from WhatsApp, email and phone enquiries is handled, and how to use your rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Status as on 14 November 2026.

1. Who is responsible

Ryan Vaz, FCA, proprietor of Vaz & Associates, F-201, Mangalmurti Apartments, L.T. Road, Opp. Maxus Mall, Borivali West, Mumbai 400092, decides why and how your personal data is used (the Data Fiduciary under the Act). For any question about your data, or to use your rights, contact Ryan Vaz at ryan@vazassociates.in (subject "Data request") or on WhatsApp at +91 63664 46161.

Please do not send PAN, Aadhaar, bank account numbers, passwords or OTPs by WhatsApp; if documents are needed for an engagement, a secure way to share them will be agreed with you.

3. WhatsApp replies and the use of AI

Replies to WhatsApp enquiries may be drafted with the help of an AI tool (Claude, provided by Anthropic). Ryan reads every message, and every reply is approved, edited or rejected by him before it is sent; the AI cannot send anything on its own. Before any text is passed to the AI tool, PAN, Aadhaar and other ID numbers, bank account and card numbers, IFSC codes and email addresses are masked. Anthropic processes the text only to prepare the draft, under commercial terms that do not allow it to use the data to train its models.

On WhatsApp you can reply STOP to stop AI-assisted replies (Ryan will reply personally if needed) or DELETE to have your messages erased.

4. Who it is shared with

Personal data is processed on our behalf by these service providers (Data Processors), only for the purposes above and under contracts that require them to keep it secure:

  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform): delivery of WhatsApp messages.
  • Anthropic: preparation of draft replies (see section 3).
  • Cloudflare: hosting of the message approval system and its database.
  • Google: website analytics.

These providers may process data outside India. Section 16 of the Act permits this except to countries the Government restricts by notification; none of these providers is in such a country as on the date above. Personal data is otherwise disclosed only where the law requires it (for example, a lawful demand from a tax authority) or with your consent. As a Chartered Accountant, Ryan Vaz is also bound by the confidentiality requirements of the Chartered Accountants Act, 1949 and the ICAI Code of Ethics.

5. How long it is kept

  • WhatsApp enquiries: a conversation with no activity for 90 days is closed, and erased one year after the last message, unless you become a client or ask for earlier erasure.
  • If you become a client, records are kept for the periods required by law and professional rules, as set out in the engagement letter.
  • Security and activity logs: one year, as required by the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025. These logs do not contain message text.
  • Backups of the message system are overwritten within 30 days.

6. How it is protected

Data is encrypted in transit and stored encrypted. Access to WhatsApp conversations is limited to Ryan Vaz and staff he authorises, each with an individual login and role; every approval, edit and deletion is logged. If a personal data breach occurs, you and the Data Protection Board of India will be informed as the Act and Rules require.

7. Your rights

Under sections 11 to 14 of the Act you can ask for:

  • a summary of the personal data held about you and how it is used;
  • correction, completion or updating of your data;
  • erasure of your data, unless the law requires it to be kept;
  • withdrawal of any consent you have given, as easily as you gave it;
  • a response to a grievance about how your data has been handled;
  • the nomination of a person to act for you in the event of death or incapacity.

To make a request, write DELETE or STOP on WhatsApp, or email ryan@vazassociates.in with the subject "Data request". Requests are answered within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with the response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

8. Children

The website and WhatsApp enquiries are meant for adults. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to contact Ryan Vaz instead.

9. Changes to this notice

This notice is updated when the way data is handled changes. The date at the top shows the current version.
