Vasai

Work for clients in Vasai

Vasai is in Palghar district, under the Vasai-Virar City Municipal Corporation. Vasai East has a large concentration of small and medium manufacturing and industrial units, and many of them work on a job-work basis for larger companies. That brings specific compliance needs: GST on job work, ITC-04, stock and costing records, TDS, and accounts that support bank finance. Vasai West has long-settled communities, many with family members working abroad.

  • GST reconciliation and notices: GST for manufacturers, job work and ITC-04, and GSTR-2B reconciliation
  • Accounts writing and bookkeeping: Books, stock records and finalisation of accounts for industrial units
  • Fractional CFO advisory: Part-time CFO support: costing, working capital and bank relationships
  • NRI taxation: NRI tax, property sales and remittances for families with members abroad
  • AI and automation for finance: Automation of stock, costing and reconciliation reports in Excel or Python

What GST compliance applies to job work?

Goods sent to a job worker can move without payment of GST under a challan, subject to time limits for return. The principal reports such movements in ITC-04, and records must show what was sent, received back and consumed.

Can you help an industrial unit prepare for a bank loan?

Yes. Work includes cleaning up books, preparing financial statements, projections and CMA data, and the stock statements banks require for working capital limits.

How are records shared if you are not in Vasai?
