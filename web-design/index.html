Questions about website work

How long does it take to build a website?

It depends on the number of pages, the features needed and how quickly content and approvals come in. A professional profile website is usually much quicker than a portal or web application. The timeline is agreed in writing at the start, with a preview link so progress can be reviewed along the way.

Who owns the website, domain and code?

The client does. The domain is registered in the client's name, and the content, design and source code are handed over at launch, along with access to hosting and Google Search Console.

Can you build a website for a chartered accountant or other professional firm?

Yes. Websites for chartered accountants and other professionals are built to respect their professional body's rules, such as the ICAI website guidelines for CA firms, including the required disclaimer and the restrictions on solicitation, testimonials and fee information.

Will the website show up on Google?

Each site is built with clean, fast pages, structured data (schema.org), a sitemap and Google Search Console set up and verified. For local searches such as a service near a particular area, a Google Business Profile matters as much as the website, and guidance is given on setting it up. No ranking position can be guaranteed.

Do you also maintain websites after launch?

Yes. Content updates, security and technical checks, and changes to structured data and search settings can be handled after launch, under a scope agreed in writing.

How much does a website cost?

Pricing depends on the number of pages, the features and any integrations. After a free initial discussion you receive a written quote with the scope, timeline and price, so there are no surprises. Ongoing maintenance can be quoted as a monthly plan.
