How to read this calendar

From 1 April 2026 the Income-tax Act, 2025 applies, and the year 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027 is called tax year 2026-27. Returns for FY 2025-26, which fall due during 2026, are still filed for that earlier year. Form numbers have changed under the new Act, so familiar names are shown with a description; check the current form on the e-filing portal before filing.

Where a due date falls on a holiday, portals usually accept filings on the next working day, but this is not guaranteed for every form. Due dates are also extended from time to time by notification. The dates here reflect the position as understood on the date of update.

Questions about due dates

When is the third instalment of advance tax due for tax year 2026-27?

The third instalment is due on 15 December 2026. By that date, at least 75 per cent of the estimated tax for the year should have been paid, after deducting TDS and TCS. The final instalment, bringing the total to 100 per cent, is due on 15 March 2027.

What is the last date for filing a belated income tax return for FY 2025-26?

A belated return for FY 2025-26 can be filed up to 31 December 2026, with a late fee and interest where tax is payable. A return already filed can be revised up to 31 March 2027, with a fee if revised after 31 December 2026.

What is the due date for GSTR-9 for FY 2025-26?

The annual return GSTR-9, and the reconciliation statement GSTR-9C where applicable, for FY 2025-26 are due by 31 December 2026, unless extended by notification.

By which date must TDS be deposited?

TDS deducted in a month must be deposited by the 7th of the following month. Tax deducted in March is due by 30 April. TDS on the purchase of property and certain other payments by individuals is paid through a challan-cum-statement within 30 days from the end of the month of deduction.

When are quarterly TDS statements due?

For tax year 2026-27, quarterly TDS statements are due on 31 July 2026, 31 October 2026, 31 January 2027 and 31 May 2027. Quarterly TCS statements are due on the 15th of the month following each quarter.
