Questions about locations

Which areas does Vaz & Associates serve?

Clients come mainly from the western suburbs and the areas north of Mumbai: Borivali, Gorai, Kandivali, Charkop, Malad, Goregaon, Dahisar, Mira Road, Bhayandar, Vasai, Nalasopara and Virar. Income tax, GST, NRI tax and automation work is also handled remotely for clients elsewhere in India and abroad.

Do I need to visit the office?

No. Most work is done on documents shared by email or a shared folder, with meetings by phone or video call. Clients who prefer to meet can visit the Borivali West office by appointment.

Can you act for me if I live outside Mumbai or outside India?

Yes. Income tax and GST compliance is done online, so a chartered accountant can act for clients anywhere in India. Non-resident Indians in the Gulf, East Africa, the United States, the United Kingdom and elsewhere are served remotely for their Indian tax matters.
