Kandivali

Work for clients in Kandivali

Kandivali West has dense residential areas around Mahavir Nagar, Charkop and Poisar, while Kandivali East includes Thakur Village and commercial and industrial pockets along Akurli Road and the Western Express Highway. Many clients here are owner-run businesses that have grown to the point where monthly accounts, GST compliance and cash planning need more attention than a year-end visit.

  • Accounts writing and bookkeeping: Bookkeeping and finalisation of accounts for traders, shops and service businesses
  • GST reconciliation and notices: GST returns, ITC reconciliation with GSTR-2B and IMS, and notice replies
  • Fractional CFO advisory: Part-time CFO support for growing businesses: cash flow, MIS and bank reporting
  • AIS and Form 26AS reconciliation: Income tax returns with AIS and Form 26AS matching for salaried and self-employed taxpayers
  • NRI taxation: NRI taxation, property sales and Form 15CA/15CB for remittances
  • Co-operative housing society accounting and audit: maintenance billing, annual accounts, statutory audit and rectification for societies in the area

Engagements are handled directly by the principal, supported by articled assistants. See all services and the compliance calendar for upcoming due dates.
