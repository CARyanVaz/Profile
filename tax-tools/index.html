Which regime is the default for tax year 2026-27?

The new regime is the default. An individual without business income can choose the old regime in the return each year. One with business or professional income can switch back to the old regime only once.

Up to what income is no tax payable under the new regime?

A resident individual with total income up to Rs 12 lakh pays no tax under the new regime because of the rebate. For a salaried person, the standard deduction of Rs 75,000 takes this to Rs 12.75 lakh of salary. Income taxed at special rates, such as capital gains, does not get the rebate.

Advance tax: instalments and interest

Enter the estimated tax for tax year 2026-27 after TDS and TCS, and what you have paid by each date.

Who has to pay advance tax?

Anyone whose tax for the year, after TDS and TCS, is Rs 10,000 or more. A resident senior citizen with no business or professional income is exempt.

What are the advance tax due dates?

15% of the tax by 15 June, 45% by 15 September, 75% by 15 December and 100% by 15 March. Taxpayers under presumptive taxation for business or profession can pay the full amount by 15 March.

How much TDS does a buyer deduct when buying property from an NRI?

For property held for more than 24 months, 12.5% plus surcharge and 4% cess, on the full sale price unless the seller has a lower deduction certificate. For property held for 24 months or less, the rate is 30% plus surcharge and cess.

Does the buyer need a TAN?

From 1 October 2026, a buyer who is a resident individual or HUF can deduct and deposit TDS on property bought from a non-resident using a PAN-based challan-cum-statement, without a TAN. Companies and firms still need a TAN.

Interest on late TDS or GST payment

Date of payment or credit to the payee, whichever is earlier.

For GSTR-3B, usually the 20th of the next month.

What is the interest on late TDS?

1% a month from the date tax should have been deducted to the date it was deducted, and 1.5% a month from the date of deduction to the date of deposit. A part of a month counts as a full month.

What is the interest on late GST payment?

18% a year on the tax paid late through the cash ledger, counted in days from the day after the due date to the date of payment.
