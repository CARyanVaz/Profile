GST notices: what each form means and how to reply

A guide to the common GST notices, the time allowed for each, and what a reply needs. Written by Ryan Vaz, FCA, a Fellow Chartered Accountant in Borivali West, Mumbai. Replies are handled in person or remotely.

Status as on 5 November 2026.

The date printed on your notice governs. Notices are also issued on the portal, so check Services, User Services, View Notices and Orders regularly, even if nothing has arrived by post or email.

Section 73 covers cases without fraud: the order is due within 3 years of the annual return due date, and the notice at least 3 months earlier. Section 74 covers fraud, wilful misstatement or suppression: the order is due within 5 years, and the notice at least 6 months earlier.

From FY 2024-25, section 74A replaces both. The notice is due within 42 months of the annual return due date, and the order within 12 months of the notice, which can be extended by up to 6 months. For FY 2024-25, with the annual return due on 31 December 2025, the notice date works out to 30 June 2029.

Periods can differ where a court stay or a special notification applies, so check the dates against your own case.

Approach

How a notice is handled

Read the notice

The form, section, tax period, amount and reply date are noted first, and the notice is checked on the portal.

Reconcile the period

GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, GSTR-2B, e-way bills and the books are compared for that period, so that each point in the notice can be accepted or explained with figures.

Reply point by point

The reply is filed on the portal in the correct form with supporting documents, and a personal hearing is requested on a show cause notice. Tax that is genuinely short-paid is paid with interest and reported by DRC-03.

Follow through

Orders are reviewed against the reply. Where the order does not deal with the points made, the appeal time limit and pre-deposit are worked out early.

Records to keep ready

  • The notice, with any earlier correspondence on the same period
  • GST portal login, or GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, GSTR-2B and GSTR-9 downloads
  • Sales and purchase registers, invoices, e-way bills and bank statements

Fees

Fees depend on the scope of work, the volume and condition of records and the time required, and are confirmed in writing in an engagement letter before work begins.

Questions about GST notices

What should I do first when I receive a GST notice?

Note the form number, section, tax period, amount and reply date, and check that the notice appears on the GST portal under View Notices and Orders. Then compare the figures with your books and returns. Do not ignore it or pay only because a notice has arrived: first work out whether the difference is real. A missed date can lead to an order without your side being heard.

How do I reply to an ASMT-10 notice?

ASMT-10 lists differences found on scrutiny of your returns under section 61. Reply in Form ASMT-11 within 30 days of service, or any further period the officer allows. Accept and pay what is genuinely short-paid, using DRC-03, and explain the rest with a reconciliation and documents. If the explanation is accepted, the officer closes the matter in ASMT-12. If not, proceedings under section 73, 74 or 74A can follow.

What is DRC-01A, and do I have to reply?

DRC-01A is an intimation that an officer may send before a show cause notice, under rule 142(1A), stating the tax, interest and penalty the officer has worked out. It is an opportunity to settle or correct the matter early. Reply in Part B by the date given, with payment through DRC-03 or your submissions. The officer may not issue it in every case, because it has been optional for officers since 2020.

How do I reply to a DRC-01 show cause notice?

DRC-01 is the summary of a show cause notice. Reply in DRC-06 on the portal by the date in the notice, dealing with each ground, attaching reconciliations and documents, and asking for a personal hearing. Under section 75(4), a hearing must be given where you ask for one or where an adverse decision is contemplated, and Circular 258/04/2026-GST says officers may grant up to four hearings. Read the attached notice as well as the summary, since the grounds are usually set out there.

What is the difference between DRC-01B and DRC-01C?

DRC-01B (rule 88C) is issued when tax reported in GSTR-1 exceeds tax paid in GSTR-3B beyond a set limit. DRC-01C (rule 88D) is issued when ITC claimed in GSTR-3B exceeds ITC available in GSTR-2B beyond a set limit. In both, you have 7 days to pay or explain in Part B. If you do neither, filing of GSTR-1 or IFF for later periods is blocked until you respond.

What is the time limit for a GST notice?

For FY 2023-24 and earlier, section 73 orders are due within 3 years of the annual return due date, with the notice at least 3 months earlier, and section 74 orders within 5 years, with the notice at least 6 months earlier. From FY 2024-25, section 74A allows a notice within 42 months of the annual return due date and an order within 12 months of the notice. FY 2022-23 section 73 orders are due by 31 December 2026.

Can the penalty on a GST notice be reduced by paying early?

Yes. Under section 73, paying tax and interest before the notice or within 30 days of it means no penalty, otherwise the penalty is the higher of 10% of tax or Rs 10,000. Under section 74, the penalty is 15% if paid before the notice, 25% within 30 days of it, 50% within 30 days of the order, and otherwise 100%. Under section 74A, the periods are 60 days: no penalty in non-fraud cases, and 15%, 25% or 50% in fraud cases.

Why did I get a notice for a GSTR-2B and GSTR-3B mismatch?

Credit is available only where the supplier has reported the invoice and it appears in your GSTR-2B, under section 16(2)(aa). Claiming more in GSTR-3B than GSTR-2B shows can bring DRC-01C, ASMT-10 or a show cause notice. The reply needs an invoice-wise reconciliation showing timing differences, credits taken in another period and supplier failures, with payment or reversal of credit that cannot be supported. GST reconciliation.

What happens if I do not reply to a GST notice?

The officer can pass an order on the available record, summarised in DRC-07. Under section 78, the amount is generally due within 3 months of service of the order, after which recovery under section 79 can begin, including from your bank account, debtors or property. The officer can require earlier payment in the interest of revenue, for reasons recorded in writing.

How do I appeal against a GST order in DRC-07?

File Form APL-01 within 3 months of the order being communicated. The appellate authority can allow one more month for sufficient cause. You must pay the admitted amount in full and 10% of the disputed tax, capped at Rs 20 crore. For an order demanding only a penalty, the pre-deposit is 10% of the penalty. Once the appeal is filed with the pre-deposit, recovery of the balance is stayed under section 107(7).

Can I get a GST notice after my registration is cancelled?

Yes. Under section 29(3), cancellation of registration does not affect liability for tax and other dues for the period before cancellation, so notices can still be issued within the time limit. Keep your portal login, email and mobile number active and keep your records, because notices continue to be issued on the portal.

Is a GST notice issued for tax below Rs 10,000?

Not by central GST officers, under CBIC Instruction 02/2026-GST dated 30 October 2026. They may not issue a show cause notice under section 73, 74 or 74A where the amount is below Rs 10,000, counting CGST, SGST or UTGST, IGST and cess together. State officers are covered only if the State issues a similar instruction. The instruction is interim, so answer any notice you do receive in time, and cite the instruction in your reply where it applies.

What must a section 74 fraud notice show?

CBIC Circular 258/04/2026-GST dated 26 October 2026 says allegations of fraud, wilful misstatement or suppression must rest on specific evidence and intent to evade tax, set out in the notice. Non-payment or a discrepancy alone is not enough. If fraud is not established, the demand is to be confined to the non-fraud provisions. The circular also calls for reasoned orders, and an order cannot exceed the notice or rest on new grounds under section 75(7).
