Virtual fractional CFO for businesses across India and abroad

Part-time CFO work done online, for businesses in any Indian city and outside India. Ryan Vaz, FCA, has more than 25 years of CFO and finance controller experience in India, Uganda and Tanzania, and works with owners, boards and accounts teams through cloud accounting, shared files and video meetings. Status as on 7 November 2026.

Where

Not limited to Mumbai

The practice is based in Borivali West, Mumbai, but virtual CFO work does not depend on location. Clients can be anywhere with an accounting system that can be reached online.

  • India: Bengaluru, Delhi NCR, Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai, Ahmedabad, Kolkata, Goa and other cities and towns
  • Abroad: Singapore and South-East Asia, the UAE and the Gulf, the UK, the US, Australia and East Africa. See international clients
  • Foreign companies with an Indian subsidiary or branch. See foreign companies in India

Meetings are fixed for the client's working hours. Visits are arranged where needed, such as a lender meeting, a board meeting or a stock review.

Scope

What the work covers

  • Thirteen-week cash flow forecast and working capital review
  • Monthly MIS pack with a short commentary, delivered on a fixed day after month end
  • Annual budget, rolling forecast and variance analysis
  • Bank and lender reporting: CMA data, stock statements, renewal packs and covenant tracking
  • Investor and board reporting, and data rooms for fundraising
  • Month-end close calendar and checklists for the accounts team, internal controls and audit readiness
  • Costing, pricing and product, branch or customer profitability
  • Financial models for plans, loans and fundraising. See financial modelling
  • Choice of accounting system or ERP, and automation of routine reports. See automated bookkeeping

Approach

How remote CFO work runs

Access

Individual logins to the accounting system and bank statements, and a shared folder on the client's drive.

First month

Review of the books, reporting, cash, banking and team, and a written list of priorities.

Monthly rhythm

Close calendar with the accounts team, cash forecast update, MIS pack, and a review call with the owners or board on a fixed date.

Tools

Zoho Books, QuickBooks Online, Xero, Tally Prime, SAP and Oracle Fusion; Excel, Google Sheets, Python and VBA for reports and reconciliations.

Background

CFO and finance controller roles in edible oil refining, cement, retail and highway construction, including raising USD 35 million of bank financing and leading a 30-person finance team. See fractional CFO advisory and experience and expertise.

Fees

Questions about virtual fractional CFO work

Is there a virtual fractional CFO in India who works with businesses outside Mumbai?

Vaz & Associates, the practice of Ryan Vaz, FCA, works as a virtual and fractional CFO for businesses anywhere in India, including Bengaluru, Delhi NCR, Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai, Ahmedabad and Kolkata, and for businesses abroad in Singapore, the UAE, the UK, the US, Australia and East Africa. The work is done online through cloud accounting, shared files and video meetings, with visits arranged where a lender or board meeting needs one.

What is a virtual fractional CFO?

A virtual fractional CFO is a senior finance leader who works for a business for part of each month and does the work mainly online. The business gets cash forecasts, monthly MIS, budgets, controls and bank or investor reporting without appointing a full-time CFO.

What does a virtual CFO do each month?

A typical month covers the close calendar with the accounts team, the cash forecast, the MIS pack with commentary, a review meeting with the owners or board, and any lender, investor or audit work due that month. The deliverables and review dates are fixed in the engagement letter.

Can a virtual CFO work with an accounts team in another city?

Yes. The CFO supervises the in-house team or outsourced accountant online, sets the close calendar and checklists, and reviews their work. Access to the accounting software, bank statements and a shared folder is usually enough.

Which accounting systems does a virtual CFO need?

Cloud systems such as Zoho Books, QuickBooks Online and Xero make remote work simplest. Tally Prime with remote access or regular data backups also works, as do ERPs such as SAP and Oracle Fusion.

Can a virtual CFO in India work with a business outside India?

Yes. Reporting can follow the business's own currency and accounting framework, meeting times are set for the client's time zone, and Indian tax, FEMA and transfer pricing matters can be handled where the business has an Indian connection.

How is client data kept secure in virtual CFO work?

Access is given through the client's own systems with individual logins, files are shared through the client's drive where possible, and access is removed at the end of the engagement. Confidentiality is covered in the engagement letter.

How is a virtual CFO different from the statutory auditor?

The virtual CFO works as part of management; the statutory auditor gives an independent opinion and cannot take on management roles for the same client. The two roles should be held by different firms.
