Income tax returns and refunds: forms, dates and corrections

Who must file, which form to use, and how to correct a return or recover a refund. Written by Ryan Vaz, FCA, a Fellow Chartered Accountant in Borivali West, Mumbai.

Questions on returns and refunds

Who is required to file an Income-tax return?

Companies and firms must file regardless of income or loss. Individuals, HUFs, AOPs and BOIs must file if income exceeds the basic exemption limit, which is Rs 4 lakh in the new regime and Rs 2.5 lakh in the old regime. Filing is also required for certain high-value spending and deposits, to carry forward business or capital losses, and for residents with foreign assets.

Which ITR form is applicable to different categories of taxpayers?

ITR-1 is for residents with simple salary, one house and other income, up to Rs 50 lakh. ITR-2 is for individuals and HUFs with no business income, including capital gains. ITR-3 is for business or professional income. ITR-4 is for presumptive income of residents. ITR-5 covers firms and LLPs, ITR-6 companies, and ITR-7 trusts and specified bodies.

What is the difference between an original, belated, revised and updated return?

An original return is filed by the due date. A belated return is filed after it, with a late fee. A revised return corrects an original or belated return. An updated return discloses omitted income later, with additional tax. For FY 2025-26, see the table on this page.

Under what circumstances can an updated return be filed?

To report income omitted or wrongly reported, including where no return was filed, within 48 months from the end of the following year. Tax, interest and additional tax of 25%, 50%, 60% or 70% must be paid first, depending on the delay. It is not allowed in some cases, such as after search. Since 2026 it can also follow a reassessment notice, at rates 10 percentage points higher.

Can an updated return be filed to reduce tax liability or claim a refund?

No. An updated return cannot reduce tax, or create or increase a refund. From 2026 it can reduce a reported loss. If you have overpaid tax, use a revised return within its time limit. After that, the only routes are narrow ones, such as an application for condonation of delay.

What are the consequences of filing an incorrect ITR?

The return may be adjusted at processing, or selected for scrutiny or a notice. Tax, interest and penalty can follow, generally 50% of the tax for under-reporting and 200% for misreporting. A knowingly false return can lead to prosecution. Correct the error early with a revised or updated return, as voluntary correction usually limits the cost.

How can an assessee rectify an intimation under section 143(1)?

Compare it with your return, AIS and Form 26AS. For a mistake apparent from the record, file an online rectification on the portal (section 287, formerly section 154). If your return had the error, revise it, if time allows, or rectify using the correct data. If it involves a real dispute, consider an appeal within 30 days. See a wrong demand.

What is the procedure for claiming an Income-tax refund?

Claim it in the return, and pre-validate a bank account linked to your PAN. Verify the return within 30 days. The refund is issued after processing, and you can track its status on the portal. Check any notice about adjustment against old demands, because that can hold up the refund.

What are the provisions relating to interest on delayed refunds?

Interest is generally 0.5% a month or part of a month. If the return was filed on time, it usually runs from 1 April after the year, and if filed late, from the filing date, up to the date of refund. No interest applies if the refund is under 10% of the tax payable, and any delay caused by the taxpayer is excluded.

How should AIS and Form 26AS mismatches be reconciled?

Download both, along with your bank, broker and TDS records. Match each item by payer, amount and date. Separate genuine income from duplicates and wrong entries, and report the correct figure in the return. Keep the working, as it is the basis for any later reply. See AIS and Form 26AS reconciliation.

What is the procedure for correcting incorrect information in AIS?

Log in to the AIS portal, choose the entry and give feedback, such as income belonging to another person, duplicate, or not fully correct. The information may then go to the source for confirmation. Feedback does not change Form 26AS or your TDS credit, so a wrong TDS entry must be corrected by the deductor.

How are refunds adjusted against outstanding tax demands?

The department can set a refund off against an outstanding demand, after giving you notice (formerly section 245). Reply online within the time given, usually 30 days: agree, disagree with reasons, or mention an appeal or stay. If the demand is wrong, say so with documents, otherwise the refund may be adjusted.

What are the consequences of non-verification of an Income-tax return?

A return not verified within 30 days of filing is treated as not filed. You may then lose the refund, or face a late fee and interest, and some losses cannot be carried forward. The date of verification can be treated as the date of filing if you are late. A request for condonation is possible where there is genuine cause.
