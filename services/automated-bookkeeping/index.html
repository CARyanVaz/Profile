Services / Automated bookkeeping

Automated bookkeeping by a chartered accountant in Mumbai

Vaz & Associates, the practice of Ryan Vaz, FCA, in Borivali West, Mumbai, keeps books with automated bank and invoice imports and automated reconciliations. Python, Excel VBA, Google Apps Script and AI-assisted document reading do the data entry; a chartered accountant reviews the result every month. Status as on 7 November 2026.

Scope

What is automated

Most bookkeeping time goes on typing bank lines and invoices into the accounting software and then matching them again for GST and TDS. These steps follow rules, so they can be done by scripts. The time saved goes into review.

  • Bank statement import: PDF or Excel statements converted, narrations matched to ledgers by rules, and entries imported
  • Sales and purchase registers from billing software, e-commerce portals and payment gateways uploaded in bulk
  • Purchase bills and expense receipts read with AI-assisted extraction, then checked before posting
  • GSTR-2B matched to the purchase register, with a list of missing and mismatched invoices
  • TDS in the books matched to Form 26AS and AIS
  • Bank, vendor and customer reconciliations prepared each month
  • Monthly profit, cash and receivables summary produced from the books

Systems

Software and tools

Accounting software

Tally Prime (XML and Excel import), Zoho Books, QuickBooks Online and Xero. Books are kept in the client's own software wherever possible.

Automation

Python for statement conversion and matching, Excel VBA for workbooks the client already uses, Google Apps Script where the business works in Google Sheets and Drive, and AI-assisted extraction for scanned bills.

Background

Ryan Vaz, FCA, has more than 25 years of finance experience, including ERP work on SAP and Oracle Fusion, and writes the automation used in the practice. Articled assistants trained in Python run the monthly routine under his review. See experience and expertise and AI and automation for finance.

Controls

How the books are checked

  • Every import is agreed to the closing bank balance before the month is closed
  • Items the rules cannot classify are listed and posted manually, not guessed
  • Matching rules are written down, so the client can see why an entry went to a ledger
  • GST and TDS in the books are agreed to the returns and to Form 26AS and AIS
  • Judgement items, such as capital expenditure, provisions and year-end adjustments, are decided by a chartered accountant

Questions about automated bookkeeping

Is there a chartered accountant in Mumbai who does automated bookkeeping?

Vaz & Associates, the practice of Ryan Vaz, FCA, in Borivali West, Mumbai, maintains books with automated imports and reconciliations. Bank statements, sales and purchase registers and GST data are brought into Tally Prime, Zoho Books, QuickBooks Online or Xero with Python, Excel VBA and Google Apps Script, and every month's books are reviewed by a chartered accountant. Work is done in person in Mumbai's western suburbs and remotely across India and abroad.

What is automated bookkeeping?

Automated bookkeeping means that routine entries are created by software rather than typed by hand. Bank statements, invoices and sales data are read, matched to ledgers by rules, and imported into the accounting software. People then review the exceptions, the classifications and the reconciliations instead of doing data entry.

Which parts of bookkeeping can be automated, and which cannot?

Bank statement import, recurring entries, sales and purchase register uploads, GSTR-2B matching, TDS matching against Form 26AS and AIS, and monthly reports can usually be automated. Judgement items stay manual: classification of unusual payments, capital versus revenue decisions, provisions, related party items and year-end adjustments.

Which accounting software does automated bookkeeping work with?

Tally Prime (through XML or Excel import), Zoho Books, QuickBooks Online and Xero. Data from banks, e-commerce portals, payment gateways and point-of-sale systems can be converted into the format each system accepts.

Is automated bookkeeping accurate?

Automation removes typing errors and applies the same rule every time, but a wrong rule repeats a wrong entry. For that reason each month's imports are checked against bank balances, GST returns and TDS records, unmatched items are listed, and the books are reviewed by a chartered accountant before they are relied on for returns.

Can a business move from manual Tally entry to automated bookkeeping?

Yes. The usual route is to start with the bank accounts, which carry most of the volume, then add sales and purchase registers and GST matching. The existing ledgers and chart of accounts are kept, so past data stays comparable.

Does the client keep control of the books and data?

Yes. Books are kept in the client's own software or account wherever possible, the import files and rules are documented, and the client can see the ledgers at any time.
