Services / Digital transformation and technology advisory

Digital transformation and technology advisory

Advice on moving finance and business processes onto better systems and digital tools, from ERP and cloud accounting to management dashboards and a firm's online presence. Digital change works when it starts from the process, not the software.

Scope

What the work covers

Businesses often buy software to solve a problem that is really about process: unclear approvals, duplicate data entry, or reports nobody trusts. The work begins by mapping how information moves through the business today, where it is re-keyed, where controls are missing and where decisions wait for data.

From that map, a phased roadmap is agreed. Some steps are small, such as automating a report or moving a register online. Others are larger, such as changing accounting software or implementing an ERP, where selection, data migration and testing need careful planning.

  • Review of finance processes, systems and controls, with a phased digital roadmap
  • ERP and cloud accounting selection, from Tally Prime and Zoho Books to QuickBooks Online, SAP and Oracle Fusion
  • Data migration planning, chart of accounts redesign and opening balance validation
  • Management dashboards and reporting built on reliable data
  • Integration of banking, GST and payroll data with the accounting system
  • Digital presence: professional websites structured for search engines and AI answer tools
  • Websites for CA practices that follow ICAI website guidelines

Approach

How the work is done

Map the current state

Processes, systems, reports and pain points are documented through short interviews and a review of actual records.

Agree the roadmap

Options are compared on cost, risk and effort, and a phased plan is agreed with clear outcomes for each stage.

Support delivery

Vendor selection, migration, testing and user training are supervised from the finance side, so the system fits the way the business actually works.

Stabilise

After go-live, reports and controls are checked against the old system until the business is confident in the new one.

Getting started

Records to keep ready

  • A list of current software, users and approximate costs
  • Samples of key reports and the spreadsheets used alongside the accounting system
  • Any vendor proposals already received

Questions about digital transformation and technology advisory

Where should a small business start with digital transformation?

Start with the process that costs the most time or causes the most errors, often month-end reporting, receivables follow-up or purchase approvals. A small, visible improvement builds confidence for larger changes.

Should we move from Tally to a cloud accounting system?

It depends on how many people need access, from where, and how much integration with banking, inventory or other systems is needed. Tally Prime remains a strong choice for many Indian businesses; cloud systems help when remote access and integrations matter more.

What causes ERP implementations to fail?

Common causes are unclear requirements, poor data migration, too much customisation, and too little involvement from the people who will use the system. Finance-side oversight of design, migration and testing reduces these risks.
