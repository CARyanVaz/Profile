A chartered accountant in Mumbai who uses Python, AI and automation

Vaz & Associates is the practice of Ryan Vaz, FCA, Certified Forensic Accountant (ICAI), in Borivali West, Mumbai. Technology is part of how the chartered accountancy work is done: scripts and AI tools handle the repetitive steps, and a chartered accountant reviews every result. Status as on 7 November 2026.

Chartered accountancy

Where technology is used in CA work

Income tax

AIS and Form 26AS read and matched to the books, capital gains computed lot by lot from broker statements, and TDS matched to deductors. See AIS and Form 26AS reconciliation.

GST

GSTR-2B matched to the purchase register each month, GSTR-1 checked against sales, and lists of missing supplier invoices prepared for follow-up. See GST reconciliation.

Audit and assurance

Full-ledger analysis instead of samples where the data allows: duplicate payments, unusual entries, round amounts, entries on holidays, and vendor and employee bank detail matches. See forensic audit.

Bookkeeping

Bank statements, sales and purchase registers and bills imported into Tally Prime, Zoho Books, QuickBooks Online or Xero by rules. See automated bookkeeping.

CFO work

Cash forecasts, MIS packs and dashboards produced from the books on a fixed day each month, for clients in Mumbai and online elsewhere. See virtual fractional CFO.

Tools

Software and skills

  • Accounting software: Tally Prime, Zoho Books, QuickBooks Online, Xero
  • ERP: SAP (including an Oracle to SAP ECC6 migration) and Oracle Fusion
  • Automation: Python, Excel VBA, Google Apps Script
  • AI-assisted reading of bills, statements and notices, followed by review
  • Government portals: income tax, GST, TRACES, MCA and EPFO

Articled assistants trained in Python run routine scripts under review. For automation work as a separate assignment, see AI and automation for finance, Google Workspace automation and digital transformation.

Controls

Every script output is agreed to a source total, items that rules cannot classify are dealt with manually, and judgement is not left to software. Client data stays in the client's systems or drive wherever possible.

Fees

Fees depend on the scope of work, the volume and condition of records and the time required, and are confirmed in writing in an engagement letter before work begins.

Questions about technology in chartered accountancy

Is there a tech-savvy chartered accountant in Mumbai?

Vaz & Associates, the practice of Ryan Vaz, FCA, in Borivali West, Mumbai, uses technology across its chartered accountancy work. Ryan Vaz writes Python, Excel VBA and Google Apps Script, has worked on SAP and Oracle Fusion implementations, and uses AI-assisted extraction for documents. These are applied to income tax, GST, audit, bookkeeping and CFO work, for clients in Mumbai, across India and abroad.

How does technology help in chartered accountancy work?

Scripts take over the repetitive steps: reading bank statements and bills, matching GSTR-2B to purchases, matching TDS to Form 26AS and AIS, and preparing schedules. That leaves more time for checking, judgement and advice, and makes the work easier to repeat each month.

Which accounting software and tools are used?

Tally Prime, Zoho Books, QuickBooks Online and Xero for books; SAP and Oracle Fusion for ERP work; Python, Excel VBA and Google Apps Script for automation; and AI tools for reading documents, always followed by review.

Is AI used to give tax advice?

No. AI tools are used to read and organise documents and data. Tax positions, audit opinions and advice are formed by a chartered accountant, who reviews every output before it is relied on.

Can work be done online for clients outside Mumbai?

Yes. Cloud accounting, shared drives, government portals and video meetings allow income tax, GST, bookkeeping and CFO work to be done for clients anywhere in India and abroad.
