AI, automation and technology

AI, automation and technology advisory

Technology services informed by 25 years of finance work: the automation is built around how accounts, tax and reporting actually run.

AI and automation for finance

Applying AI tools and automation to accounting work: reading documents, matching entries and producing reports with far less manual effort, always under chartered accountant review.

AI is most useful in finance when it does the repetitive reading, sorting and matching, and a qualified professional checks the result. Workflows are designed so that every figure stays traceable to its source document and client data is handled under professional confidentiality.

Typically covers

  • AI-assisted extraction from invoices, bank statements, AIS, Form 26AS and other PDFs
  • Automated reconciliations with exception reports for review
  • AI-assisted drafting of summaries and working papers, checked before use
  • Python and Excel VBA automation for recurring reports and schedules
  • Review of where AI fits in an existing finance process, and the controls it needs

Google Workspace automation (Apps Script)

Custom Google Apps Script tools that connect Sheets, Gmail, Drive, Forms and Calendar, so routine office and finance tasks run on their own.

Many businesses already run on Google Sheets and Gmail. Apps Script adds automation inside those tools without new software licences, and the scripts are documented so the business can maintain them after handover.

Typically covers

  • Automated MIS and reports built in Google Sheets
  • Filing of invoices and documents from Gmail into organised Drive folders
  • Compliance calendars with scheduled reminder emails
  • Form-based approval workflows, such as purchase requests
  • Custom menus, sidebars and dashboards inside Sheets

Digital transformation and technology advisory

Advice on moving finance and business processes onto better systems and digital tools, from ERP and cloud accounting to a firm's online presence.

Digital change works when it starts from the process, not the software. The work maps how information moves through the business today, identifies where systems, data or controls fall short, and plans and supports the change in practical stages.

Typically covers

  • Review of finance processes, systems and controls, with a phased digital roadmap
  • ERP and cloud accounting selection, migration and clean-up support
  • Management dashboards and reporting built on reliable data
  • Digital presence: professional websites structured for search engines and AI answer tools
  • Websites for CA practices that follow ICAI website guidelines

Finance, tax and assurance

Chartered accountancy services

Fractional CFO advisory

Senior finance leadership on a part-time basis, for businesses that need CFO-level input without a full-time appointment.

A fractional CFO works with the management team for a set number of days each month. The aim is to give owners reliable numbers, a clear view of cash, and a finance function that can support lenders, investors and auditors.

Typically covers

  • Cash flow planning and working capital management
  • Budgets, rolling forecasts and monthly management reports (MIS)
  • Information packs for banks, lenders and investors
  • Month-end close, internal controls and audit readiness
  • Finance systems and ERP selection or clean-up

Forensic audit

An independent, evidence-based examination of books and transactions where there are concerns about irregularities, leakage or misstatement.

Forensic work establishes facts. It traces transactions through documents and data, identifies patterns that ordinary audits may not look for, and records findings in a form that management, boards or legal counsel can rely on.

Typically covers

  • Transaction trail and vendor or payment pattern reviews
  • Data analytics on ledgers to flag anomalies and duplicates
  • Physical verification support for inventory and fixed assets
  • Review of internal control gaps that allowed the issue
  • Structured fact-finding reports

GST and tax reconciliation (AIS / 26AS)

Matching what the tax and GST portals show against your books, so that returns and filings agree with each other.

Tax portals now hold detailed third-party data about every taxpayer. Reconciliation compares that data with your own records, explains each difference, and corrects what needs correcting before it becomes a notice.

Typically covers

  • GSTR-2B and Invoice Management System (IMS) against the purchase register
  • GSTR-1, GSTR-3B and books of account, month by month
  • Annual Information Statement (AIS) and Form 26AS against books and returns
  • Tracing of missing TDS and TCS credits
  • Feedback on incorrect AIS entries and support with notice replies

NRI taxation

Indian tax compliance for non-residents and returning Indians, from residential status to property sales and treaty relief.

Non-residents are taxed in India only on Indian income, but the rules on residency, withholding and repatriation are detailed. The work starts with determining status for the year and then applies the correct treatment to each income source.

Typically covers

  • Residential status, including RNOR status for returning Indians
  • Tax on NRE and NRO interest, rent, dividends and capital gains
  • Property sales, TDS on sale and lower deduction certificates
  • Tax treaty (DTAA) relief, Tax Residency Certificate and Form 10F
  • Form 15CA / 15CB for remittances and income tax return filing

Accounts writing and bookkeeping

Accurate books of account, maintained month by month, so that financial statements, tax returns and reports rest on clean records.

Good books are the base for everything else: tax filings, bank submissions and management decisions. Records are kept to a documented routine with regular reconciliations, so year-end finalisation is a review rather than a reconstruction.

Typically covers

  • Bookkeeping in Tally Prime, Zoho Books, QuickBooks Online and similar software
  • Bank, vendor and customer ledger reconciliations
  • Books of account for professionals, traders and small businesses
  • Finalisation of accounts and year-end schedules
  • Records organised for audit and tax filing

Financial modelling

Structured spreadsheet models that turn business assumptions into projected financial statements, cash flow and valuation.

A model is useful only if others can follow and test it. Models are built with separated inputs, calculations and outputs, clear labelling and checks, so owners and lenders can see how each figure is derived.

Typically covers

  • Integrated three-statement models (profit and loss, balance sheet, cash flow)
  • Project finance models with debt service coverage analysis
  • Budgets, scenarios and sensitivity analysis
  • Business plans prepared for bank or investor review
  • Review of existing models for errors and logic
