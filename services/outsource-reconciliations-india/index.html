International clients / Outsourcing reconciliations

Can a business outside India outsource its reconciliations to India?

Yes. Reconciliations are one of the easiest parts of finance work to move to a remote team, because they run on statements and ledgers that already sit in the cloud. Written by Ryan Vaz, FCA, a Fellow Chartered Accountant in Mumbai who has run finance teams in India, Uganda and Tanzania.

Status as on 13 November 2026.

Summary

What can be reconciled remotely

ReconciliationSource documentsUsual frequency
Bank and cashBank statements or feeds for every account and currencyMonthly, or weekly for high volumes
Corporate cards and expensesCard statements, receipts, expense claimsMonthly
Payment gateways and marketplacesStripe, PayPal, Shopify, Amazon payout and fee reportsMonthly
Suppliers and customersSupplier statements, customer ledgers, ageing reportsMonthly or quarterly
IntercompanyLedgers of each group entity, exchange ratesMonthly, before consolidation

How the work is set up

The work is done in your own accounting system, such as QuickBooks Online, Xero, Zoho Books, NetSuite or Tally, with a user login you control. Bank and platform statements are shared through bank feeds, read-only access or a secure shared drive. Payments and approvals stay with your team. A written engagement letter sets out the reconciliations covered, the monthly calendar, confidentiality and who reviews what.

What you receive each month

  • Completed reconciliations for each account, signed off by date
  • A list of unmatched and old items, with the likely reason for each
  • Proposed correcting entries, posted after your approval or posted directly if agreed
  • A short note on recurring problems, such as missing receipts or duplicate entries

Where automation comes in

For a few dozen transactions a month, bank rules in the accounting software are usually enough. For hundreds or thousands, for example card payments settled through a gateway in batches with fees deducted, a Python, Excel VBA or Google Apps Script routine matches transactions by amount, date and reference. Only the exceptions are left for review, and the routine is documented and handed over so that it keeps working if the engagement ends.

Worked example

An online retailer in Dubai sells through its own Shopify store and Amazon, collects through Stripe, and banks in dirhams and US dollars. Each month:

StepItemsResult
Stripe payouts matched to bank receipts2,400Matched by script; fees and refunds split out
Shopify orders matched to Stripe charges2,350Unmatched orders listed for the store team
Amazon settlement reports matched to bank4Settlements, fees and reserves posted
Exceptions reviewed by hand35Explained, corrected or carried forward

The figures are illustrative. What matters is that most of the volume is handled by the matching routine, and review time goes to the few items that need judgement.

What stays with local professionals

Statutory audits, tax returns and other filings that your country reserves for locally licensed professionals remain with them. The reconciliations and schedules prepared remotely are the working papers they rely on, and work is coordinated with them at year end. Questions on outsourcing reconciliations

Can reconciliations be outsourced to an accountant in India?

Yes. Bank, card, ledger, intercompany and payment-gateway reconciliations can all be prepared remotely, working in your own accounting system with a login you control and statements shared through a secure drive. The reconciliations follow your month-end calendar and your local accounting conventions.

Which reconciliations are usually outsourced?

The most common are bank and credit card reconciliations, supplier statement and customer balance reconciliations, intercompany balances between group entities, and payouts from Stripe, PayPal, Shopify, Amazon and other platforms matched to sales recorded in the books.

What access is needed?

A user login in your accounting system, such as QuickBooks Online, Xero, Zoho Books, NetSuite or Tally, and read-only access to bank and platform statements or monthly exports of them. Payments and approvals stay with your own team; the reconciler does not need payment rights.

How does automation help with reconciliations?

Where there are hundreds or thousands of transactions a month, a Python, Excel VBA or Google Apps Script routine matches items by amount, date and reference, so that only unmatched items need human review. The routine and its documentation are handed over, so your team can run it too.

What remains with local professionals?

Statutory audits, tax returns and other filings that your country reserves for locally licensed professionals stay with them. The reconciliations and schedules prepared remotely are the working papers they rely on.

Is Indian GST charged on reconciliation work for a client outside India?

Usually not. Services supplied from India to a client outside India and paid for in foreign currency generally qualify as export of services, which is zero-rated under Indian GST. Whether a particular engagement qualifies depends on the facts, such as where the client is located and how payment is received.
