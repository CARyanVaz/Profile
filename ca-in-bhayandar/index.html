Does it matter that Mira-Bhayandar is in Thane district and not Mumbai?

For income tax and GST, a chartered accountant can act for you wherever you are in India. Local registrations such as professional tax and shop licences follow the rules of the area where the business operates.

Can a first-time home buyer claim tax benefits on a home loan?

Interest and principal on a home loan can qualify for deductions, subject to limits and conditions, under the old tax regime. Under the new regime most of these deductions are not available, so the choice of regime should be compared each year.
