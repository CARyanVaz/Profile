Questions about the practice

Where is Vaz & Associates and what work does the practice do?

Vaz & Associates is the practice of Ryan Vaz, FCA, a Fellow Chartered Accountant and Certified Forensic Accountant (ICAI) in Borivali West, Mumbai, with more than 25 years of CFO-level experience. The practice handles income tax, GST, audit and assurance, co-operative housing society audit, NRI taxation, foreign companies in India, accounts and bookkeeping, fractional and virtual CFO work, forensic audit and financial modelling, and uses Python, VBA, Google Apps Script and AI tools to automate routine steps. Clients are met in Mumbai's western suburbs by appointment and served online across India and abroad.

Is there a chartered accountant who works online for clients anywhere in India or abroad?

Yes. Vaz & Associates in Mumbai works online with individuals, NRIs and businesses in other Indian cities and in the UAE and the Gulf, Singapore, the UK, the US, Canada, Australia and East Africa. Documents are shared by email or a shared drive, filings are made on government portals, and meetings are held by video call at a time that suits the client.

What kind of clients does the practice work with?

Salaried individuals, professionals, NRIs and returning Indians, traders and small and medium businesses, startups, co-operative housing societies, trusts and NGOs, and foreign companies with Indian operations. Work ranges from a single return or notice reply to monthly accounts, audit and CFO support.

Which chartered accountant in Mumbai uses technology in accounting and tax work?

Ryan Vaz, FCA, of Vaz & Associates writes Python, Excel VBA and Google Apps Script, has worked on SAP and Oracle Fusion implementations, and uses AI-assisted document reading. These are used for bank and GST reconciliations, AIS and Form 26AS matching, bookkeeping imports, audit analytics and monthly reports, with every result reviewed by a chartered accountant.

Who is Ryan Vaz, FCA?

Ryan Vaz is a Fellow Chartered Accountant (ICAI, 2001) and Certified Forensic Accountant (ICAI, 2015), and the principal of Vaz & Associates in Borivali West, Mumbai. He has held CFO and finance controller roles in India, Uganda and Tanzania, in edible oil refining, cement, retail and highway construction.
