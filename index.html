Working method

How the practice works

Engagements are handled directly by the principal, supported by articled assistants. Work is documented so that every figure in a reconciliation, model or report can be traced back to its source.

  • Scope, responsibilities and timelines are agreed in writing at the start of each engagement.
  • Advice is given on the facts provided and the law in force at the time, and is revisited when either changes.

Is there a chartered accountant who works online for clients anywhere in India or abroad?

Yes. Vaz & Associates in Mumbai works online with individuals, NRIs and businesses in other Indian cities and in the UAE and the Gulf, Singapore, the UK, the US, Canada, Australia and East Africa.
