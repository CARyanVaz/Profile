News: changes in GST, income tax and other laws

Notifications, circulars and advisories as they are issued, with the date they take effect and a link to the detail. GST and income tax are listed separately. Compiled by Ryan Vaz, FCA, Borivali West, Mumbai.

Status as on 13 November 2026. For due dates, see the compliance calendar; for questions and answers, see Knowledge and FAQ.

GST

DateChangeReferenceIn force from
4 Nov 2026E-way bill numbers to go from 12 to 14 digits
Consolidated e-way bill numbers go from 10 to 11 digits. Businesses using ERP systems or APIs should update fields and validation rules; the change appears in the sandbox first.		GSTN advisory dated 4 November 20261 January 2027
30 Oct 2026No further arrests, no notice below Rs 10,000, limits on e-way bill interception
Instruction to GST officers following the 57th GST Council meeting.		Instruction 02/2026-GSTIn force
28 Oct 2026Classification of goods and GST on certain services
Two clarificatory circulars on classification and on the GST treatment of certain services.		Circulars 265 and 266Issued
26 Oct 2026Post-sale discounts: credit note under section 34, no prior agreement needed
Brings sections 153 to 155 of the Finance Act, 2026 into force. A discount given after supply is excluded from taxable value if the supplier issues a credit note under section 34 and the recipient reverses the related ITC; the earlier condition of an agreement linked to specific invoices no longer applies. The same notification brings in the refund changes.		Notification 03/2026-Central Tax1 November 2026
26 Oct 2026New REG-01 and REG-14, rule 14B for e-commerce sellers, revised cancellation, refund rules
Revised registration and amendment forms, registration in a State through an e-commerce operator's warehouse, system approval of compliant REG-16 applications, and faster refund acknowledgement. Revised REG-16 and RFD-01 apply from 1 December 2026.		Notification 05/2026-Central Tax1 November 2026, with later dates for some forms
26 Oct 2026GST TDS on purchases of specified scrap above Rs 2.5 lakh
A registered buyer deducts 2% GST TDS on used cooking oil, plastic and tyre scrap, metal scrap and e-waste bought from a registered supplier where the contract value exceeds Rs 2.5 lakh.		Notifications 06/2026 and 07/2026-Central Tax1 November 2026
26 Oct 2026Registration, demand and appeals, pre-deposit, ISD, section 17(4) and export receipts
Circular 257 gives officers document lists for registration and limits on queries; Circular 258 allows up to four personal hearings on a show cause notice.		Circulars 257 to 264Issued

DateChangeReferenceIn force from
1 Oct 2026Resident individual or HUF buying property from an NRI can deduct TDS without a TAN
The buyer deducts and deposits the TDS using a PAN-based challan-cum-statement. Companies and firms buying from an NRI still need a TAN.		Income-tax Act, 20251 October 2026
6 Jul 2026CBDT FAQs on the move to the Income-tax Act, 2025
Proceedings for tax years before 1 April 2026 continue under the 1961 Act; section 12AB and 80G registrations already granted remain valid.		CBDT FAQs dated 6 July 2026Issued
