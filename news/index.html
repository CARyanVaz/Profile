News: changes in GST, income tax and other laws

Notifications, circulars and advisories as they are issued, with the date they take effect and a link to the detail. GST and income tax are listed separately. Compiled by Ryan Vaz, FCA, Borivali West, Mumbai.

Status as on 21 November 2026. For due dates, see the compliance calendar; for questions and answers, see Knowledge and FAQ.

GST

DateChangeReferenceIn force from
20 Nov 2026Metro Rail added as a mode of transport in e-way bills
Metro Rail can now be selected as the mode of transport when generating an e-way bill, updating Part-B or the vehicle, and for consolidated e-way bills. A transport document number must be entered when Metro Rail is selected.		GSTN advisory dated 20 November 2026; rule 138(2A) of the CGST Rules as amended by Notification 05/2026-Central TaxRule from 1 November 2026; web facility available now, API in sandbox
17 Nov 2026E-invoices for reverse charge self-invoices: live from 15 December 2026, required from 1 January 2027
Self-invoices issued under reverse charge for supplies from unregistered persons (RCMDOM) and for imports of services (RCMIMP) are to be reported on the invoice registration portals for an IRN, with scope and exemptions as set out in the notifications. The facility is proposed to go live on 15 December 2026 for documents dated from that day, is voluntary until 31 December 2026 and mandatory from 1 January 2027.		GSTN advisory dated 17 November 2026; Notifications 04/2026 and 05/2026-Central Tax1 January 2027 (voluntary from 15 December 2026)
4 Nov 2026E-way bill numbers to go from 12 to 14 digits
Consolidated e-way bill numbers go from 10 to 11 digits. Businesses using ERP systems or APIs should update fields and validation rules; the change appears in the sandbox first.		GSTN advisory dated 4 November 20261 January 2027
1 Nov 2026Registration amendments other than principal place of business approved on submission
Changes such as legal name, partners or directors and additional places of business now amend the certificate on submission, after OTP verification of the authorised signatory and one promoter within 3 days and online checks of PAN, DIN and CIN. A change of principal place of business is still examined by the officer.		GSTN advisory dated 1 November 2026; rule 19 of the CGST Rules substituted by Notification 05/2026-Central Tax1 November 2026
30 Oct 2026No further arrests, no notice below Rs 10,000, limits on e-way bill interception
Instruction to GST officers following the 57th GST Council meeting.		Instruction 02/2026-GSTIn force
28 Oct 2026Classification of goods and GST on certain services
Two clarificatory circulars on classification and on the GST treatment of certain services.		Circulars 265 and 266Issued
26 Oct 2026Post-sale discounts: credit note under section 34, no prior agreement needed
Brings sections 153 to 155 of the Finance Act, 2026 into force. A discount given after supply is excluded from taxable value if the supplier issues a credit note under section 34 and the recipient reverses the related ITC; the earlier condition of an agreement linked to specific invoices no longer applies. The same notification brings in the refund changes.		Notification 03/2026-Central Tax1 November 2026
26 Oct 2026New REG-01 and REG-14, rule 14B for e-commerce sellers, revised cancellation, refund rules
Revised registration and amendment forms, registration in a State through an e-commerce operator's warehouse, system approval of compliant REG-16 applications, and faster refund acknowledgement. Revised REG-16 and RFD-01 apply from 1 December 2026.		Notification 05/2026-Central Tax1 November 2026, with later dates for some forms
26 Oct 2026GST TDS on purchases of specified scrap above Rs 2.5 lakh
A registered buyer deducts 2% GST TDS on used cooking oil, plastic and tyre scrap, metal scrap and e-waste bought from a registered supplier where the contract value exceeds Rs 2.5 lakh.		Notifications 06/2026 and 07/2026-Central Tax1 November 2026
26 Oct 2026Registration, demand and appeals, pre-deposit, ISD, section 17(4) and export receipts
Circular 257 gives officers document lists for registration and limits on queries; Circular 258 allows up to four personal hearings on a show cause notice.		Circulars 257 to 264Issued

Income tax and TDS

DateChangeReferenceIn force from
1 Oct 2026Resident individual or HUF buying property from an NRI can deduct TDS without a TAN
The buyer deducts and deposits the TDS using a PAN-based challan-cum-statement. Companies and firms buying from an NRI still need a TAN.		Income-tax Act, 20251 October 2026
28 Sep 2026Tax audit report due 21 October 2026 and audit-case returns due 21 November 2026
For taxpayers whose accounts must be audited, the audit report date moved from 30 September to 21 October 2026 and the return due date from 31 October to 21 November 2026. Returns with a transfer pricing report remain due on 30 November 2026.		CBDT Circular 07/2026 dated 28 September 2026Assessment year 2026-27 (FY 2025-26)
6 Jul 2026CBDT FAQs on the move to the Income-tax Act, 2025
Proceedings for tax years before 1 April 2026 continue under the 1961 Act; section 12AB and 80G registrations already granted remain valid.		CBDT FAQs dated 6 July 2026Issued

Companies Act, LLP, labour and other laws

DateChangeReferenceIn force from
27 Oct 2026Cost audit report (CRA-4) for FY 2025-26: no additional fee up to 31 December 2026
Form CRA-4, the cost audit report in XBRL, for FY 2025-26 can be filed without additional fees up to 31 December 2026.		MCA General Circular 06/2026 dated 27 October 2026Up to 31 December 2026
24 Oct 2026Annual filings for FY 2025-26: AOC-4 up to 31 December 2026, MGT-7 and MGT-7A up to 31 January 2027
Financial statements in AOC-4, AOC-4 CFS, AOC-4 XBRL and the NBFC Ind AS versions can be filed without additional fees up to 31 December 2026, and annual returns in MGT-7 or MGT-7A up to 31 January 2027. The time for holding the annual general meeting is not extended.		MCA General Circular 05/2026 dated 24 October 2026Without additional fees up to 31 December 2026 (AOC-4) and 31 January 2027 (MGT-7, MGT-7A)
