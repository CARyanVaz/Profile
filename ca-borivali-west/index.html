Fees and careers

Fees, articleship and vacancies

How fees are set

Fees depend on the scope of work, the volume and condition of records, deadlines and the time required. The scope and fee are confirmed in writing in an engagement letter before work begins. A fee list is not published, in keeping with ICAI guidelines.

Articleship and roles for freshers

The firm takes articled assistants and trains them in audit, tax, GST and accounting work, with exposure to Python-based automation. ICAI students eligible for articleship, and commerce graduates looking for an accounts role, can email a CV to ryan@vazassociates.in. Openings are filled as they arise.
