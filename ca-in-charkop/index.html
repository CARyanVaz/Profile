Charkop

Work for clients in Charkop

Charkop combines large residential sectors with the Charkop industrial estate, where small manufacturing units, job workers and traders operate. That mix shapes the work: salaried and retired residents need clean income tax returns and AIS matching, while units in the industrial estate need monthly GST, TDS and books that stand up to a bank or an assessing officer.

  • Accounts writing and bookkeeping: Monthly books for units and traders in the Charkop industrial estate, in Tally Prime or Zoho Books
  • GST reconciliation and notices: GSTR-1, GSTR-3B and GSTR-2B reconciliation, and replies to GST notices
  • AIS and Form 26AS reconciliation: Income tax returns with AIS and Form 26AS matching for salaried, retired and self-employed residents
  • NRI taxation: NRI tax for families whose members live abroad, including sale of a Charkop flat by a non-resident
  • AI and automation for finance: Excel and Python automation for stock, costing and job-work records
  • Co-operative housing society accounting and audit: maintenance billing, annual accounts, statutory audit and rectification for societies in the area

Engagements are handled directly by the principal, supported by articled assistants. See all services and the compliance calendar for upcoming due dates.
