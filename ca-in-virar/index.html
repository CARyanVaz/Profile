What is the QRMP scheme in GST?

The Quarterly Return Monthly Payment scheme lets eligible small taxpayers file GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B quarterly while paying tax monthly through PMT-06. It reduces filing work for small businesses, though invoices can still be uploaded monthly through IFF so customers get their credit on time.

Do I need to file a return if my salary TDS is already deducted?

Yes, if your income is above the basic exemption limit. Filing also lets you claim refunds, carry forward losses and report other income such as interest, which AIS will show in any case.
